pMap

Map. Edit. Visualize.

A plasmid map and sequence editor that runs entirely in your browser. Open SnapGene, GenBank, FASTA and EMBL files, design primers, plan digests and export publication-ready maps.

or drop .dna, .gb, .fasta, .embl, .ab1 or session files anywhere in this window

Open anything

SnapGene .dna with features, primers, notes and colors, plus GenBank, FASTA and EMBL. Several files at once.

Edit and annotate

Type, paste and recolor bases. Features move with every edit, and copy/paste carries them between files.

Design primers

PCR and sequencing primers with nearest-neighbour Tm, hairpin and dimer checks, 5′ tails and PCR simulation.

Digest and gel

115 common enzymes, unique-cutter views, virtual digests and a simulated agarose gel with ladders.

Find and auto-annotate

Exact or similar DNA, protein search, and automatic annotation from a feature library or pLannotate output.

Verify and share

Align Sanger .ab1 traces to check a construct. Save maps as SVG or PNG and sequences as SnapGene .dna, GenBank or FASTA.
